# ExecPlans

When you tackle complex features, risky refactors, or cross-cutting changes, author an ExecPlan before you start coding. ExecPlans follow the rules in .agent/PLANS.md and must be kept up to date until the work is finished.

Use an ExecPlan whenever a task touches multiple packages, alters security-critical flows (API key pipeline, BTCPay integration, authentication), or seems likely to span more than one focused coding session. Reference the ExecPlan during implementation and keep its progress, decisions, and surprises current.
